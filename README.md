I love coding and while my normal style is web design, app coding was a great challenge.

This app is not directly deployed from the app stores for respective phones because of developer charges, but it should still download on your phone!

For iPhones, open the app link and hit the share button at the bottom of the screen. Scroll down the menu and tap "add to home screen"
For android, open the app link and hit the 3 dots in the top right corner. Tap "add to home screen" as well

I made this app because I see people who impulsively spend their money so much! It is important early on to see the importance of saving money and resisting impulse buys.
This app gamifies that concept and I hope people will use it to help them save money.
